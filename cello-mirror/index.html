The elbow stays at one height and the wrist travels in a straight line. That is the picture to copy. Use "Show model arm" on the Live mirror to put it over your own shoulder.
Feasibility test. Load a clip of the left hand (side view, highest frame rate your phone allows). The page steps through it frame by frame, runs the hand model, and reports how often it finds the hand while the picture is slow, medium and fast moving.

Bands are thirds of the clip ranked by how much the picture changes between frames, so they do not depend on the model. "Unstable" means the finger length jumped sharply from one frame to the next, a sign the model guessed.

Top strip: green = hand found, red = hand lost. Bars below: how much the picture is moving.
