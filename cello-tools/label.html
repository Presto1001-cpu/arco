Fingertip labelling tool

Load the video and the manifest file, then click 7 points on each frame: the wrist, the knuckle at the base of the middle finger, and the five fingertips. Everything stays on your computer.

Click on the exact centre of the fingertip, even if it is slightly blurred. If a fingertip is hidden by the neck or another finger, press X for that point. If no left hand is in the picture, press S. Your progress is saved in this browser as you go.
